Changes: this is not the original. BistroExterior.fbx from Bistro_v5_2.zip was
reduced from 2.83 million to about 325 thousand triangles by meshoptimizer's
simplifier, no surface moved more than 5 cm (40 cm for the leaf, hedge and ivy
cards), each repeated object reduced once and its copies placed from it; only the base-colour textures are kept, downscaled to at most 256
pixels and saved as PNG; normal, specular and emissive maps, the interior
scenes and the environment map are dropped. Made with
launcher/tools/r3d/source/reduce_source.py:

    python launcher/tools/r3d/source/reduce_source.py BistroExterior.fbx launcher/demo/bistro/source \
        --name bistro --error 5 --error-for "Foliage_*Leaves*=40" \
        --error-for "Foliage_Bux_Hedges*=40" --error-for "Foliage_Ivy_leaf*=40" \
        --texture-size 256 --source-up Y
