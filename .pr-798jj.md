Bistro's fits now train on probe views over the whole street, with the camera path as added views, instead of on `street.anim.toml` alone. Probes are the standard, and a known path adds data (798jj, policy of 11-15). A new visibility source, `probes`, gives each fitted start what the fit's own probe faces show, plus the path when the scene camera has one. Stacked on #1594 (tkkf4 normals), which the probe fit needs to stay finite.

|---|---|---|
